# Assessment: Complex Arithmetic, Analytic Functions, and Contours

Complete all four problems. Justify analyticity on an open set, not merely at
one point. No solutions are provided.

## Problem 1 — Polar algebra

Let $z=2e^{i\pi/4}$ and $w=3e^{-i\pi/3}$.

1. Compute $z^2/w$ in polar and rectangular forms.
2. Find all fourth roots of $-16$.
3. Determine the principal argument of $z^2/w$.
4. Explain how the answer changes if arguments are represented modulo $2\pi$.

## Problem 2 — Analyticity and derivatives

Let $f(z)=x^3-3xy^2+i(3x^2y-y^3)$ and $g(z)=|z|^2e^{iz}$.

1. Use the Cauchy–Riemann equations to analyze $f$.
2. Identify $f$ as a standard complex power and compute $f'(z)$.
3. Write real and imaginary parts of $g$.
4. Determine where $g$ is complex differentiable and whether it is analytic
   anywhere.

## Problem 3 — Contour integrals

Let $\gamma_R(t)=Re^{it}$, $0\le t\le2\pi$, with $R>0$.

1. Compute $\oint_{\gamma_R}z^n\,dz$ for every integer $n\ge0$.
2. Compute $\oint_{\gamma_R}z^{-2}\,dz$ directly.
3. Compute $\oint_{\gamma_R}(\bar z+z^2)\,dz$.
4. Relate the result to the area enclosed by the circle.

## Problem 4 — Cauchy's theorem and path dependence

Let $\gamma$ be a positively oriented rectangle enclosing $0$ but not $2$.

1. Evaluate $\oint_\gamma dz/(z-2)$.
2. Evaluate $\oint_\gamma dz/z$.
3. Explain which domains make each integrand analytic and why the answers
   differ.
4. Give a path from $1$ to $3$ and compare it with a path winding once around
   $2$, explaining the change in value.
