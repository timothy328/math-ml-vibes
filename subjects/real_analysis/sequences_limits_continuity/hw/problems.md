# Real Analysis — Topic 1 Homework

## Problem 1 — Sequence limits (Easy)

Prove directly from the epsilon definition that $a_n=3/n$ converges to 0.
Your proof must specify an explicit choice of $N(\varepsilon)$.

## Problem 2 — Algebra of limits (Easy)

Assume $a_n\to a$ and $b_n\to b$.

1. Prove $a_n+b_n\to a+b$.
2. State the additional condition needed to prove $a_nb_n\to ab$.
3. Explain where boundedness enters the product proof.

## Problem 3 — Function limits (Medium)

Use an epsilon-delta proof to show

$$
\lim_{x\to2}(x^2+3x)=10.
$$

Choose a preliminary restriction on $|x-2|$ before selecting $\delta$.

## Problem 4 — Continuity and uniform continuity (Medium)

1. Prove that $f(x)=x^2$ is continuous at every real number.
2. Prove that $f(x)=x^2$ is uniformly continuous on $[0,1]$.
3. Explain why the same proof does not establish uniform continuity on all
   of `R`.

## Problem 5 — Compactness and stability (Challenge)

Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ be continuous.

1. Prove that `f` is bounded.
2. Prove that `f` attains a maximum and a minimum.
3. If $g$ is $K$-Lipschitz and $|x-y|\leq\varepsilon$, bound
   $|g(x)-g(y)|$.
4. Interpret the bound as a perturbation guarantee for a model score.
